Privacy Policy for Metronome Pro

Last updated: December 2024

Overview

Metronome Pro is a browser extension that provides rhythm and timing tools for musicians. This privacy policy explains how we handle user data.

WE COLLECT NO PERSONAL DATA WHATSOEVER

Data Collection

Metronome Pro does NOT collect any personal data.

Our extension:

  • Does NOT collect any personal information
  • Does NOT track user behavior
  • Does NOT send any data to external servers
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking tools
  • Does NOT require user accounts or registration
  • Does NOT access your browsing history
  • Does NOT monitor your activity outside the extension

Local Storage

The extension uses your browser's local storage solely to save your metronome preferences, including:

  • Tempo settings
  • Time signature preferences
  • Volume levels
  • Sound selections
  • Accent patterns
  • Polyrhythm configurations
This data:
  • Remains completely on your device
  • Is never transmitted anywhere
  • Is never shared with third parties
  • Can be cleared by removing the extension

Third-Party Services

Metronome Pro does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or external websites. The extension operates entirely offline.

Permissions

The extension requests only one permission:

  • Storage: Used exclusively to save your metronome settings locally on your device

Data Security

Since no data is collected, transmitted, or stored on external servers, there are no security risks related to data breaches or unauthorized access to personal information. Your settings remain private and secure on your device.

Children's Privacy

Our extension is safe for users of all ages as no personal data is collected from anyone, including children under 13.

International Users

Since we collect no data, GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations do not apply to our data practices. Your privacy is protected regardless of your location.

Changes to Privacy Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be updated in this document. Since no data is collected, changes will primarily be clarifications to this policy.

Data Retention

We retain no data as we collect no data. Settings stored locally on your device persist until you remove the extension or clear your browser data.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository or Chrome Web Store listing.

Summary

Metronome Pro is designed with privacy in mind - we simply don't collect any user data at all. Your musical practice remains completely private.
